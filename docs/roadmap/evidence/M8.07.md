## Policy implemented

- The read-only update plan keeps `current`, `wanted`, and `latest` compatibility classifications separate. Patch/minor classification only nominates a candidate; it does not claim runtime compatibility.
- A candidate may be applied only at the exact current `wanted` patch/minor version from a plan bound to the exact source SHA. The executor rejects major, prerelease, unknown, missing, or stale candidates before dependency mutation.
- Major candidates now include a structured compatibility report: current/target versions, direct workspace dependents, upstream release-notes URL (or an explicit missing-data action), required API/configuration inventory, migration/rollback ownership tasks, the complete candidate validation gates, and CRITICAL dependency/image scan requirements.
- The major report explicitly remains manual-review-only. Promotion risk is independently derived from commit range and changed paths; labels are not trusted as an override. Breaking, security-sensitive, dependency, workflow, ORM, and destructive changes still require fresh CODEOWNER approval under M1.08.
- Candidate application runs the shared gate list used by the major impact report, then Board lint, container smoke tests, and blocking CRITICAL scans before candidate publication. An E2E failure stops candidate creation and publication.
