## Contract and result

- `.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs a new restore drill against its isolated PostgreSQL service. It creates a random source DB and a different random target DB, never reusing or writing to the workflow's normal test database.
- The source fixture installs Base, Auth and Jobs addons programmatically, initializes a synthetic administrator password, registers a temporary test user through the auth service, and creates linked company/partner parent-child/primary-address/company-membership records.
- A mode-0600 custom-format dump and mode-0600 expected-value file are held under a mode-0700 temporary directory. The normal `backup-postgres.sh` and `restore-postgres.sh` scripts are exercised.
- The target restore first receives an intentionally wrong confirmation. The drill requires exit status 2 and the cancellation result, verifies a target sentinel is unchanged, then removes the sentinel before supplying the exact random target name.
- The target verifier compares counts and IDs for addon identities, company, default users, fixture users, partners, addresses and memberships; checks company, parent-child, address and user-company membership relations; and authenticates the fixture account after restore.
- The runner reports dump and restore durations for its synthetic fixture. It explicitly does not infer a production RPO/RTO: off-host backup cadence, infrastructure provisioning and traffic cutover are not established by this test.

## Safety and recovery limits

- The only maintenance connection is `POSTGRES_TEST_URL`; the source and destination URLs replace its database path with two generated safe names. The fixture gets only those derived URLs.
- A random synthetic password is held in process environment and is never included in stdout, expected-value file, or evidence. Child-process diagnostics are suppressed to prevent connection-string leakage.
- Temporary databases and files are removed in `finally`; no production service or database was contacted.
- A single runner duration is a measurement, not an operational guarantee. RPO remains undefined until an encrypted off-host backup cadence and alerting policy are configured; end-to-end RTO remains undefined until provisioning and service cutover have a measured runbook.
