## Implemented

- Added a closed JSON Schema envelope with a version marker, at most 20 suggestions, and exactly three kinds: summary, term, and duplicate. Unknown properties are rejected.
- Every candidate is constrained to `unreviewed` and `INTERNAL`, with canonical suggestion, Assertion, and Evidence identifiers and bounded citation metadata.
- Citations bind exact source/edition/locator, full-source SHA-256, Unicode-scalar range, and selected-span SHA-256. The trusted resolver rechecks source identity, digest, readability, and selected-span digest.
- Summary text must exactly equal its cited spans in order; term text must exactly equal its one cited span. Duplicate suggestions require two distinct active/readable Assertions and readable active Evidence bound to each requested Assertion. The schema includes no rationale or merge instruction fields.
- `validateResearchSuggestionEnvelopeWithResolver` fails closed on malformed envelopes, unavailable or mismatched sources, invalid spans, unsupported text, unreadable/inactive records, and invalid Evidence-to-Assertion bindings. It performs validation only and has no canonical write path.
- Tests byte-check the two approved local sample artifacts and exercise malformed shapes, citation/span mismatch, unsupported text, and duplicate-binding failures.
