The current head no longer contains the values; history redaction requires an owner-approved supported recovery process because branch rules prohibit force-push.
- Promotion of `dev` into `main` remains subject to fresh CODEOWNER approval through PR #24; no merge is included in this evidence.
- Scanner output may reveal genuine advisories; they must be triaged and remediated or narrowly excepted with expiry, never hidden by broad ignores.
- Scan status is a point-in-time result and requires recurring updates; zero open alerts does not mean the repository can stop scanning.
