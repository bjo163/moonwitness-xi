## Delivered

- Added `packages/ui/src/styles/tokens.css` as the sole owner of brand and semantic color values, light/dark mappings, chart palette, typography, spacing, radius, shadows, motion, and reduced-motion tokens.
- Added [the token guide](../../../packages/ui/TOKENS.md) with contrast, semantic status, chart, font-hosting, and motion rules. The design-doc page is a pointer to this package-owned guide.
- Switched Board palette aliases and Tailwind theme entries to shared variables rather than repeated literals.
- Replaced white text on pink backgrounds with `text-on-pink`. The dark ink foreground measures 5.55:1 on brand pink; white measures 3.50:1 and fails normal-text AA.
- Added seven source-contract tests that resolve token aliases, calculate WCAG contrast for both themes, check text pairs at 4.5:1, control boundaries and focus at 3:1, and preserve stable status/chart semantics.
